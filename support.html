Common questions

My workouts aren't showing up
  • Make sure Apple Health is connected: Settings (the gear on Home) › Apple Health. If it says Not connected, tap Connect Apple Health.
  • Check that Fitness RPG is allowed to read workouts in the iPhone's Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Fitness RPG.
  • Tap Sync Workouts from Health in Train › Workouts, or Sync Now in Settings.
  • Only workouts inside the Import Window (Settings › Apple Health) are imported, and types listed under Ignored Workout Types are skipped.
  • A workout from your Apple Watch can take a few minutes to reach your iPhone's Health app.
Why did a workout earn less XP than I expected?

The workout history shows why under each workout. The usual reasons:

  • Entered by hand: workouts typed into Health earn no rewards.
  • Heart rate near resting, or no heart rate and almost no calories: the workout earns a quarter of its XP and coins, and no raid attempt or Arena ticket.
  • Long day: minutes past 90 in a day count half, and past 180 a quarter.
  • Heart-rate zone: easier sessions (Zone 1–2) earn a bit less, harder ones more.
I got a new iPhone or reinstalled the app

If iCloud Backup is on (Settings › Save Data), sign in to the same iCloud account and open the app: it offers to restore your hero. Your workout history comes back from Apple Health as it syncs to the new iPhone. You can also restore a file you saved with Export Backup using Restore from Backup.

I can't join a guild
  • Guilds need you to be signed in to iCloud and to agree to the community rules in the Guild tab.
  • You can be in one guild at a time. Leave your current guild at the bottom of its Hall first.
  • Some guilds require a minimum Combat Level, shown on the guild's card.
  • If it says you're already a member, update to the latest version and tap Join again to get your membership back.
How do I report or block another player?

Long-press a guild member or a leaderboard row to report or block that player. Long-press a guild in the guild list to report it. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I manage or cancel Fitness RPG Pro?

Pro is sold by Apple. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. For a refund, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com. If Pro isn't showing on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in Settings › Membership.

How do I get the Apple Watch app?

It installs with the iPhone app if your watch is set to install apps automatically. If not, open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Fitness RPG under Available Apps and tap Install. Workouts you start on the watch save to Apple Health and count in the game.

How do I delete my data?
  • Online data (your hero's profile, Arena records, and guild chat messages from earlier versions): Settings › Online Play › Delete My Online Data.
  • iCloud Backup: turn it off in Settings › Save Data, then delete Fitness RPG's data in the iPhone's Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
  • Everything on your devices: delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch.
