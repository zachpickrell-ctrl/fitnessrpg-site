The short version

  • Fitness RPG reads workout, activity, heart, sleep and mindfulness data from Apple Health to power your hero. That data stays on your devices and is never uploaded.
  • Online play (guilds and Arena duels against other players' heroes) runs on Apple's iCloud. Other players see your hero's name, levels, gear and Arena record, and what you write about your guild. You're identified by an anonymous ID that iCloud makes for this app, not by your name, email address or Apple Account.
  • There is no advertising, analytics or tracking. We never sell your information, and Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • You can delete your online data, turn off Health access, reset your progress or delete the app at any time.

Who we are

Fitness RPG is a role-playing game for iPhone and Apple Watch in which your real workouts level up your hero. It is made by Zach Pickrell ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the personal information described here. The app is offered in the United States, Canada and Mexico. This policy explains what information the app uses, where that information is kept and the choices you have.

Apple Health data

With your permission, the app reads the following from Apple Health. You choose which types to allow when the app asks, and you can change your answer later in the Health app.

DataWhat the game uses it for
WorkoutsType, start time, duration, calories and distance of each workout become XP, coins and other rewards. For walks, runs and hikes, whether the workout was indoors or outdoors and the elevation climbed train the Trailblazing skill.
Heart rateAverage heart rate during a workout sets its intensity zone, which scales the XP it earns.
Date of birthUsed only to estimate your maximum heart rate for those zones. Your birth date is never displayed or stored in the game.
Active energy and exercise minutesDaily totals count toward your weekly streak.
Walking, running and cycling distanceDistance goals in campaigns and records in your Hero's Journal.
Resting heart rate and VO2 maxLong-term fitness trends shown in your Hero's Journal, and monthly charts in Training Insights (read when you open it and never stored).
Sleep and mindful minutesScore the Recovery skill: hours asleep, bedtime consistency and mindful minutes each day.
StepsDaily step totals add Stamina for your hero at set step counts. Steps typed in by hand are left out.

When you start a workout from the Apple Watch app, it records the workout (with the heart rate, calories and distance your watch measures) and saves it to Apple Health, so it appears in Health and Fitness like any other workout. The watch app asks for permission to save workouts before it does this. The iPhone app never writes to Apple Health.

The game keeps a summary of each imported workout (type, time, duration, calories, average heart rate, distance, whether it was indoors, elevation climbed and the rewards it earned) and of each night's Recovery score (hours slept, bedtime and mindful minutes), and your step totals for the last few days, as part of your game progress, so you can see them in the app.

Your game progress

The app saves what you do in the game: your hero's name and appearance, skill levels, items, coins, pets, guild, settings and journal entries. This save lives on your iPhone. The parts of it that other players need to see are also shared online, as described next.

Playing online

Guilds and the Arena are played with other people. They use the app's own database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), and you need to be signed in to iCloud to use them. Everything else in the game works without it. Online play stays off until you agree to our community rules, which the app asks for in Battle › Arena and in the Guild tab. Until then, nothing about you or your hero is uploaded.

Your online ID

iCloud gives each player an ID that exists only for Fitness RPG. It doesn't contain or reveal your name, email address, phone number or Apple Account, and we can't use it to find out who you are. Everything you do online is stored under this ID.

What other players can see

DataWhy it's shared
Your heroName, appearance, title, skill levels and experience, equipped gear, pet and its skin, and the food you bring to the Arena. Other players' games fight your hero in Arena duels (where it appears on screen as it looks in your game) and show it on the leaderboard, and your guildmates can look at your hero in the guild.
Arena recordRating, wins, losses, best tier this season and the results of duels against your hero (the challenger's hero name and level, who won, and both ratings).
Activity datesWhen you first played and when you last played. The Arena uses them to match active players and to spot heroes whose levels are impossible for how long they've played.
Guild activityJoining, leaving and rank changes, what you put into or take from the guild bank, upgrades you buy for the guild, and your raid damage and attempts each week. Your guildmates see this in the guild's log. The guild's name, description and size appear in the guild list for everyone.
Guild infoThe guild's description and message of the day, if you write or change them. Members of your guild can read both, and the description also appears in the guild list.

Your skill levels come from your workouts, so they show roughly how much you train. No workout, heart rate, sleep, mindfulness or other Health data is uploaded, and neither is your date of birth.

Reports and moderation

You can report a hero name, a player you think is cheating, or a guild. A report contains the reason you chose, the reported player's online ID and a copy of the reported text (for a cheating report, their rating and level). Only you and our moderators can read it. We review reports, and we may hide guilds or block players from online play when they break the community rules. We keep reports for as long as we need them to handle them. You can also block a player so their hero's name is hidden from you. Your block list stays on your iPhone.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your Arena rating is also posted to a Game Center leaderboard, and the feats you earn are reported as Game Center achievements. Apple shows them with your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your Game Center account details.

Deleting your online data

In the app, Settings › Online Play › Delete My Online Data takes you out of your guild and deletes your hero's online profile, any guild chat messages you posted in earlier versions of the app (guild chat has been removed and they are no longer shown), your guild list entries and the duel records you created. Your raid entries lose your hero's name. The guild's shared history (for example that a member deposited coins) stays, because your guildmates' games need it, but it is linked only to your anonymous online ID. This also turns online play off, and nothing is shared again unless you agree to the community rules again. Deleting the app doesn't delete your online data, so use this option first or email us and we'll delete it for you.

Where your information is kept

  • On your iPhone. Your game progress is a file inside the app on your iPhone. We don't run servers of our own.
  • In Fitness RPG's iCloud database. What you share by playing online (see Playing online) is stored in the app's public database in Apple's iCloud, which Apple runs for us. Apple's data centers may be outside your country. We can see this database through Apple's developer tools, which we use to keep the game running and to handle reports.
  • In your own iCloud. Unless you turn it off in Settings › Save Data › iCloud Backup, the app keeps a copy of your game progress in your private iCloud storage, so a new iPhone or a reinstall can restore your hero. Only you can read it. We can't see it, and it counts against your own iCloud storage. It holds game results only, never Apple Health data: no workout times, lengths, calories, heart rate, distances, sleep or steps. After a restore, the app reads your workout history back from Apple Health on that iPhone.
  • On your Apple Watch. Your iPhone sends a small summary of your hero (activity, levels, coins and a short scene animation) to the Fitness RPG watch app over Apple's secure connection between your paired devices. When you finish a workout on the watch, the watch tells your iPhone so it can import that workout.
  • In widgets. The Home Screen widgets and watch complications read a small summary from storage that only Fitness RPG's own app, widgets and complications can access.
  • In your device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes the app's saved progress in that backup, as it does for other apps, so your hero comes back when you restore. Apple Health data itself is backed up by Apple under Health's own rules, not by the game.
  • In backups you export. In the app, Settings (the gear on Home) › Save Data › Export Backup creates a file of your game progress and hands it to you. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases and Pro

Fitness RPG Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active and remembers when it renews, so Pro works while you're offline. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example when your hero runs out of Stamina or when your streak needs a workout). They are created on your iPhone, and you can turn each one off in the app's settings.

When you play online, iCloud can also notify you about guild activity (silently, to keep your guild up to date) and when other players challenge your hero. These are sent by Apple's iCloud and push notification service. We don't run a notification server.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your information, or share it with anyone other than the players you play with online and Apple, which hosts the online database.
  • Use Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, or disclose it to third parties.
  • Upload your Health data to any server, or sync it through iCloud. The iCloud Backup of your game progress leaves Health data out. (Your own device backups, described above, are made by Apple and controlled by you.)
  • Show ads or include analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks.
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Health access. Change what the app can read at any time in the iPhone's Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Fitness RPG. The game keeps working with whatever you allow.
  • Notifications. Turn reminders off in the app's settings, or all of them in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Fitness RPG.
  • Online play. Online play is off until you agree to the community rules in Battle › Arena or the Guild tab, so you choose whether to share anything. Settings › Online Play › Delete My Online Data removes what you've shared and turns online play off again (see Playing online).
  • Reset. In the app, Settings › Save Data › Reset All Progress erases your game progress on the device, and the iCloud Backup is then replaced with the new hero. It doesn't delete your online data or take you out of your guild.
  • iCloud Backup. Turn it off in Settings › Save Data › iCloud Backup. To delete the copy already in iCloud, open the iPhone's Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage, find Fitness RPG and delete its data.
  • Delete. Deleting Fitness RPG from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes all of its data from those devices (but not your iCloud Backup, so a reinstall can restore your hero). Workouts the watch app saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Your rights

Most of what the game keeps about you never leaves your devices, and you can see it or delete it yourself in the app (see Your choices). For what is stored online, and for anything you've emailed us, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it for a particular purpose, or withdraw a consent you gave.

Email us from the address below. Tell us your hero's name so we can find your online records, which are stored under your anonymous online ID. We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you, for example by changing your hero's name to a word we send you, because we don't know who you are otherwise.

In Mexico, these are your ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation and opposition). In Canada, if you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

If a security incident ever puts your personal information at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Online play connects you with other players, so it isn't meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for online services where you live). We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared information through online play, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released, and we will describe the change in the app's release notes.
